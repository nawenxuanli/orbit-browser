# Orbit Browser privacy policy

Effective October 28, 2026.

Orbit Browser stores bookmarks, recent browsing history, open tabs, groups, and appearance preferences locally on your device. Microsoft Edge WebView2 stores website cookies, cache, and other site data in the local browser profile. Orbit does not operate a browsing-data collection service, analytics service, advertising service, account system, or synchronization service.

When you visit a website or search, your device connects to that website or your selected search provider. Those services receive normal connection information, including your IP address and the requests you make, and apply their own privacy policies. The default search provider is DuckDuckGo; Google and Bing are optional. Microsoft Edge WebView2 Runtime is a Microsoft component with its own update and data-processing behavior; see Microsoft's privacy statement at https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement.

The optional ad and tracker blocking uses a bundled static domain list. It does not provide anonymity or block every form of tracking. Orbit does not download external filter subscriptions.

Windows data is stored under `%LOCALAPPDATA%/OrbitBrowser`. You can delete visit history from History and clear cookies and website data from Privacy. Removing the app does not necessarily delete all locally stored user data. This Windows preview denies website permission requests, including camera, microphone, location, and notifications.

Orbit does not knowingly collect children's personal information through a publisher-operated service. Websites you access remain governed by their own policies.

For privacy questions or deletion assistance for local data, contact the publisher through https://github.com/nawenxuanli/orbit-browser/issues. Do not post private browsing history, credentials, or other sensitive information in public issues.
